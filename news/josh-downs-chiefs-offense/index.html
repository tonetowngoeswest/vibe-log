Analysis

Josh Downs gives Mahomes a potent new weapon

Kansas City’s deadline addition brings sharp routes and a knack for beating man coverage to an offense looking for more reliable answers.

TH

By Tony Hernandez

Chiefs News contributor

Join the conversation

Independent coverage.
Red and gold perspective.

Nov. 12, 20261 min read

Josh Downs is headed to Kansas City, and the appeal is easy to understand: Give Patrick Mahomes another receiver who can get open before a routine passing play turns into an escape room. The trade is complete. The offensive upgrade still has to happen on the field.

NFL.com reported Tuesday that the Chiefs acquired Downs and a 2027 seventh-round pick from Indianapolis for fourth- and fifth-round selections in the same draft. Kansas City has bought another option for its passing game, with the cost measured in future picks rather than wishful thinking.

The Chiefs’ own breakdown offers a useful introduction. Downs has 33 catches for 354 yards and a touchdown in nine games this season. In Week 2 at Arrowhead, he caught seven passes for 72 yards against Kansas City, with six receptions producing first downs. The audition apparently traveled well.

That team assessment identifies route running and separation against man coverage as his calling cards. The attraction for Mahomes is a target who can create a throwing window quickly. His skill set could provide an extra answer on third down and reduce the number of possessions that depend on improvisation.

Fitting him alongside Rashee Rice, Xavier Worthy and Travis Kelce is the coaching assignment. More receiving options create possibilities; they also require decisions about spacing, timing and who gets the ball. Downs will need to learn the offense, and his quarterback will need to learn his cues.

For now, the sensible excitement is about the skill set. Downs gives Kansas City another way to challenge coverage. If that becomes dependable production, the Chiefs’ deadline shopping trip will have delivered something more useful than a handsome receipt.
