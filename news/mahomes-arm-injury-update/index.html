Injury update

Mahomes arm injury update: Quarterback returns after elbow scare

Mahomes returned before halftime against the Jets; a return to play is not a medical diagnosis.

TH

By Tony Hernandez

Chiefs News contributor

Nov. 8, 2026 · Halftime reporting snapshot1 min read

Patrick Mahomes returned to the field before halftime Sunday after a hit to his right elbow briefly sent the Chiefs quarterback to the sideline against the New York Jets. That is the encouraging part. The less satisfying part: Returning to a game does not, by itself, establish how an injury will feel afterward.

The scare came on a second-quarter sack by Jets linebacker David Bailey that forced a fumble. The Jets' official highlight identified Bailey's strip-sack and Will McDonald's recovery. Yahoo Sports reported that Mahomes went to the sideline holding his right arm, threw there and returned for Kansas City's next possession.

On that drive, Mahomes completed a 12-yard pass and scrambled for 7 yards, according to Yahoo Sports' account. Kansas City reached halftime leading 14-12. Coach Andy Reid told CBS at the break that Mahomes had hurt his elbow and that it was sore, but that he had come back.

Those are observations and an initial coaching update, not a diagnosis. The sources reviewed for this halftime snapshot did not establish a specific elbow injury, imaging result or recovery timetable. A grimace can set off a thousand nervous group chats; it cannot answer those medical questions.

The Chiefs' official pregame report had listed Mahomes with a knee issue, full participation throughout the practice week and no game designation. That report predates Sunday's elbow scare. It should not be treated as evidence that the new concern has been evaluated or resolved.

For now, the useful update is narrow: Mahomes returned and continued playing before halftime. Anything more definitive should come from an attributed team update or subsequent reporting. Chiefs fans can exhale without pretending the medical chart has suddenly become public.
