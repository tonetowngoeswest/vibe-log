Injury update

Mahomes practices fully after elbow scare ahead of Atlanta

Mahomes practiced fully Wednesday and says his right elbow is improving with treatment as the Chiefs prepare to visit Atlanta.

TH

By Tony Hernandez

Chiefs News contributor

Updated Nov. 12, 2026 · Based on Nov. 11 report1 min read

Patrick Mahomes was a full participant in the Chiefs’ Wednesday practice after hurting his right elbow against the Jets on Sunday. Kansas City’s Week 10 injury report supplies the latest confirmed practice update, and it is encouraging news as the Chiefs prepare to visit Atlanta.

The official report lists Mahomes with knee and right elbow issues and marks his Nov. 11 participation as full. As of this update, the team had not published Thursday’s participation report or a final injury designation for the Atlanta game.

Speaking Wednesday, Mahomes said his elbow was improving with treatment at the facility and at home, according to KCTV5’s coverage of his news conference. He said swelling had affected how comfortable he felt throwing during the Jets game and credited trainer Julie Frymyer with helping reduce it.

Mahomes also said he felt encouraged after speaking with doctors following an MRI. He described pain management as the focus going forward, with treatment continuing throughout the week as he works toward Sunday’s game.

The scare began when Jets pass rusher David Bailey hit Mahomes’ throwing arm late in the first half of Kansas City’s Nov. 8 win. In its Monday report, KCTV5 noted that Andy Reid had described Mahomes as day to day, with rehabilitation and soreness management ahead.

Wednesday’s full practice gives Kansas City a newer checkpoint than Reid’s initial assessment. Mahomes is preparing for Atlanta while continuing treatment. The team’s remaining injury reports will provide the next updates before Sunday’s game.
