Party of six: Patrick and Brittany Mahomes expecting fourth child

The Chiefs quarterback and his wife shared their growing family's good news in a beachside announcement.

TH

By Tony Hernandez

Chiefs News contributor

Independent coverage.

Nov. 12, 20261 min read

Call it the happiest expansion team in Kansas City. Patrick and Brittany Mahomes are expecting their fourth child, with the Chiefs quarterback and his wife sharing the news in an Instagram announcement on Tuesday, Nov. 10. The family's next big addition will arrive well away from the transaction wire.

ABC News reported that the couple revealed the pregnancy with a series of family photos taken on a beach. With Sterling, Bronze and Golden in the photographs, the announcement put the whole household at the center of the news. For once, the number drawing attention around Mahomes was six, the size of the family once the baby arrives.

According to KCTV5, the new arrival will join daughter Sterling Skye, born in February 2021; son Patrick, known as Bronze, born in November 2022; and daughter Golden Raye, born in January 2025. Sterling and Bronze already know the older-sibling routine. Golden is next in line for a promotion.

The couple did not announce a due date, KCTV5 reported. That leaves the calendar open, and there is no reason to fill it with guesses. The news itself supplies plenty to celebrate without a countdown clock or a scouting report on baby names.

ABC's family profile traces the couple's relationship back to high school and their wedding to March 2022 in Hawaii. Now that shared history has another chapter ahead. Chiefs fans can debate personnel and playoff possibilities elsewhere; this particular update calls for a simple congratulations.
